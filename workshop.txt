version=1
title=DownedRP
description=Standalone knockdown, self-treatment and assisted-walk system for multiplayer roleplay.
description=
description=[h1]Knockdowns[/h1]
description=Fatal damage puts you on the ground with a timer instead of killing you. You can still crawl, treat your own wounds if your First Aid is good enough, and give up if nobody is coming.
description=
description=Get picked up and you carry a knockdown Moodle. Each one fades after a while. Take too many in quick succession and the next one kills you.
description=
description=Getting swarmed and dragged down is the exception. That kills you outright, the way it always did.
description=
description=[h1]Self-treatment[/h1]
description=Server owners decide whether a downed player may patch themselves up. Set the required First Aid level to -1 to forbid it entirely, 0 to allow anyone, or 1-10 to require that level. Once every wound is bandaged or splinted and nothing is bleeding, you can stand up on your own.
description=
description=[h1]Assisted walk[/h1]
description=After a revive the rescued player follows the rescuer at reduced speed for both of them. Walk too far apart and the hold breaks.
description=
description=[h1]Server settings[/h1]
description=Every system can be retuned or switched off completely.
description=[list]
description=[*]Knockdown length, how many you survive, revive health and revive speed
description=[*]Whether players or zombies can finish off someone who is down
description=[*]How long knockdowns take to fade
description=[*]Required First Aid level for self-treatment
description=[*]Assisted-walk follow distance, break distance and speed penalty
description=[/list]
description=
description=Counts live on the server, so logging out on the death screen will not reset them.
description=
