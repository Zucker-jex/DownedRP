The knockdown state machine, the knockdown/revive network commands, the
server-side knockdown bookkeeping, the knockdown Moodle and the
_knockedd_* animation sets in this mod are adapted from that project.
DownedRP keeps only the Knockdown module; the Wounds, Respawn and Loot
Cache modules of the original are not included.

Changes made for DownedRP:
  * Global namespace renamed from DBNO to DRP.
  * Wounds, Respawn, Snapshot, Loot Cache and Admin UI modules removed.
  * Self-treatment of wounds while downed, gated on First Aid level.
  * Self get-up once every wound is treated.
  * Assisted-walk follow system after a revive.
  * Sandbox options trimmed and re-paged under DRPMain / DRPAssist.
